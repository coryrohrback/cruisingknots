Estimated lifetime distance as of October 22, 2026
983,678 nautical miles

That is about 45.5 trips around the Earth, or about 4.7 one-way trips to the Moon. At its typical pace of about 184 nautical miles per day, it adds roughly 169 more every day it sails.

How we estimate it

We take the regions this ship sails today, apply a typical number of nautical miles per day for each, and add up the days since it entered service, with a pause for 2020 to 2021. Detours, weather diversions and past route changes are not captured, so treat the number as an estimate. Read more.
