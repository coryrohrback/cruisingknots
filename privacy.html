Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What we collect

Cruising Knots does not ask you to create an account, and it has no forms. We do not collect your name, email address or other personal details directly.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and served through the domain cruisingknots.com. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense and similar services. These services, including Google, use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Local storage

Your browser may remember small preferences on your own device, such as the layout you chose. This data stays on your device.

Links to other sites

Ship pages link to third-party sites, such as ship trackers and Wikimedia Commons. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
